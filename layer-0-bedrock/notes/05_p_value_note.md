# What p-value means / does not mean

What p-value means:
- It measures how surprising the data would be if the null hypothesis were true.
- Smaller p-value means the observed result is less likely under the null.

What p-value does NOT mean:
- It does NOT mean the probability that the null hypothesis is true.
- It does NOT prove a result is important in real life.
- It does NOT mean your model or theory is correct.

Main takeaway:
- p-value is about evidence against the null, not proof of truth.
